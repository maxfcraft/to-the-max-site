Marketing for pool service and maintenance companies

A pool service company is a route business. The value of a new customer is not the first clean, it is the number of years they stay on your route, and every advertising decision should follow from that.

01 / The problem

What goes wrong when pool service and maintenance companies advertise

Weekly service is a small ticket with a long life, and advertising it as a one-off clean makes the lead cost look impossible when it is actually one of the best buys in local service.

Equipment repair and replacement, pumps, heaters, filters, liners, is high ticket and nearly invisible in most pool companies' advertising.

Route density decides whether this business makes money, and a customer twenty minutes off the route can be worth less than nothing.

02 / The build

What To The Max builds for pool service and maintenance companies

  1. Weekly and monthly service as the headline offer, with the lifetime of the route customer as the number you judge lead cost against.

  2. Radius targeting mapped to the routes you already run, so new customers land next to existing stops.

  3. A separate equipment repair and replacement campaign, which carries the ticket that funds the year.

  4. Opening and closing campaigns timed to the season, which are a reliable entry point onto the weekly route.

  5. Retention follow-up, because a route customer lost in year two costs more than several new acquisitions.

04 / Questions

Questions pool service and maintenance companies ask

Do Facebook ads work for pool service companies?

Yes, and the recurring structure makes the maths some of the friendliest in local service. A weekly service customer who stays three years is worth several thousand dollars, so a lead cost that looks alarming against one clean is trivial against the route.

How much does a pool service lead cost?

Forty to one hundred and twenty dollars. Judge it against the lifetime of the route customer, not the first invoice. At two hundred dollars a month for three years, even a hundred and twenty dollar lead is one of the cheapest acquisitions you will ever make.

Should pool companies advertise repairs separately?

Yes. Pumps, heaters, filters, and liners carry a far larger ticket than weekly service and have a different trigger, usually something that just broke. Running them inside the service campaign means they never get budget or a fair test.

How does route density affect pool advertising?

It decides whether the work is profitable at all. A customer twenty minutes off your route can lose money on a normal service price. Target the radius around routes you already run, so every new customer is added to an existing stop rather than creating a new drive.

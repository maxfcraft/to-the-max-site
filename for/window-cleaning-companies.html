Marketing for window cleaning companies

Window cleaning has the tightest margins and the best recurring structure in exterior services. Whether it is a good business or a hard one comes down to route density and whether the customer is on a schedule.

01 / The problem

What goes wrong when window cleaning companies advertise

A one-off residential window clean is a small ticket carrying a full drive time. Advertise that alone and the math never gets comfortable, no matter how cheap the leads are.

The recurring commercial route, storefronts, offices, restaurants, is where this trade becomes an actual business, and almost nobody advertises to it because everyone is chasing homeowners.

Window cleaning is the easiest service in the world to bundle and the least bundled. The same visit could carry gutter cleaning, screen repair, and a house wash.

02 / The build

What To The Max builds for window cleaning companies

  1. Recurring schedules as the offer: twice a year residential, monthly or quarterly commercial, with a price that rewards staying on the calendar.

  2. Bundled packages built into the ad, since adding gutters or a house wash to an existing stop raises the ticket without adding drive time.

  3. A separate commercial campaign for storefronts, offices, and restaurants, where contracts are recurring and the feed is empty.

  4. Radius targeting built around the routes you already run, because in this trade drive time is the real cost of every job.

  5. Seasonal timing around spring cleaning and pre-holiday, when residential demand actually spikes.

04 / Questions

Questions window cleaning companies ask

Do Facebook ads work for window cleaning?

Yes, when the offer is a recurring schedule or a bundle rather than a single clean. A one-off residential job carries a small ticket and a full drive time, so it struggles to absorb a lead cost. A twice-yearly schedule or a bundled exterior package absorbs it easily.

How much does a window cleaning lead cost?

Fifteen to forty dollars residential, forty to one hundred and twenty commercial. The commercial lead looks expensive and is usually the better buy, because a storefront on a monthly schedule is worth several thousand dollars a year and stays for years.

Should window cleaners go after commercial or residential?

Both, in separate campaigns. Residential gives you volume and cash flow. Commercial gives you recurring revenue, predictable routes, and almost no competition in the feed. Most window cleaning companies run only the residential half and wonder why the year is lumpy.

What should a window cleaner bundle with a clean?

Gutter cleaning, screen repair, and a house wash. The truck is already there and the drive time is already paid for, so every add-on is close to pure margin. Bundling in the ad itself raises the average ticket before the customer ever speaks to you.

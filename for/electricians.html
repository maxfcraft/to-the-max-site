01 / The problem

What goes wrong when electrical contractors advertise

An hourly service call is the lowest form of electrical revenue and the hardest to advertise profitably, because there is no headline in it. Nobody stops scrolling for an hourly rate.

The project work has obvious triggers that almost nobody advertises against. A new EV in the driveway. A panel too small for a remodel. A neighbourhood that lost power for three days last winter and is now shopping generators.

Electricians are chronically undersold on trust signals. Licence, insurance, permit handling, and inspection pass rate are exactly what a homeowner is nervous about, and they almost never appear in the ad.

02 / The build

What To The Max builds for electrical contractors

  1. Project campaigns instead of service campaigns: panel upgrades, EV charger installs, whole-home generators, recessed and landscape lighting.

  2. Trigger timing. Generator campaigns after an outage event, EV charger campaigns into new-EV-owning audiences, panel campaigns into older housing stock.

  3. Licence, insurance, and permit handling stated plainly in the creative, because that is the actual objection in a trade where bad work burns a house down.

  4. A commercial and property-manager campaign running alongside, since recurring commercial maintenance is the steadiest revenue in the trade and is barely contested in the feed.

  5. Follow-up that survives a long decision. A panel upgrade can take six weeks to decide, and a lead that goes quiet is not a lead that said no.

04 / Questions

Questions electrical contractors ask

Do Facebook ads work for electricians?

For project work, clearly yes: panel upgrades, EV chargers, generators, and lighting all sell well in the feed because they are visual, high ticket, and considered. For hourly service calls the math is thin, because there is no compelling offer to put in front of someone who does not currently have a problem.

What should an electrician advertise?

Projects with a picture and a price range: panel upgrades, EV charger installation, whole-home generators, recessed lighting, and landscape lighting. Each has a visible trigger you can target against. An hourly rate has no trigger and no image, which is why service-call ads underperform in this trade.

How much does an electrical lead cost?

Fifty to two hundred dollars for project work. On a three thousand dollar panel upgrade closing at one in three, a hundred and fifty dollar lead is comfortable. The number that matters is cost per booked project, and in this trade that can look bad for three weeks and fine at week six.

Are EV charger installs worth advertising?

Yes, because the trigger is dated and targetable. Somebody who just bought an electric vehicle needs a charger within weeks and usually does not know who to call. The ticket is modest but the customer is now yours for the panel upgrade, the generator, and everything else in the house.

How long should you wait before judging an electrical campaign?

At least six weeks on project work. Panels and generators carry a two to eight week decision window, so a campaign judged at day ten looks like a failure and is usually about to convert. This is the single most common reason electricians switch off a campaign that was working.

01 / The problem

What goes wrong when siding and replacement window companies advertise

A lead is worthless here. An appointment where one spouse is home and the other is not is close to worthless too, because the decision cannot be made in the room.

These are five-figure purchases with a two to three month decision window, and most companies abandon follow-up inside two weeks.

Energy savings claims are both the strongest angle and the fastest route to a policy problem or a complaint, because most of them are exaggerated in the industry as a matter of routine.

02 / The build

What To The Max builds for siding and replacement window companies

  1. The appointment as the conversion event, with confirmation steps designed to get both decision makers in the room.

  2. Financing and monthly payment framing up front, because a fifteen thousand dollar number in an ad stops the scroll for the wrong reason.

  3. Long follow-up sequences that run for months without becoming annoying, since the decision window genuinely is that long.

  4. Honest, checkable claims about efficiency and warranty, which protects the ad account and stands up when the homeowner verifies them.

  5. Neighbourhood-level targeting on housing stock age, because a fifteen-year-old subdivision is a completely different prospect from a new build.

04 / Questions

Questions siding and replacement window companies ask

Do Facebook ads work for siding and window companies?

Yes, and they are one of the higher-ticket categories where the maths is forgiving. The discipline is optimising for in-home appointments rather than leads. Lead volume in this trade is easy to buy and almost entirely meaningless if nobody sits down at the kitchen table.

How much should a window or siding appointment cost?

One hundred and fifty to five hundred dollars per set appointment. On a fifteen thousand dollar job closing at one in four sits, even five hundred dollars per appointment leaves the campaign comfortably profitable. The lead price is irrelevant next to the sit rate.

Should the ad mention financing?

Yes, and early. A five-figure sticker price in a feed ad stops the scroll for the wrong reason. A monthly payment figure keeps the conversation alive long enough to get to an appointment, where the full number can be presented properly with the value attached to it.

How long does it take to close a siding or window job?

Four to twelve weeks is normal, and it can be longer. That is why follow-up matters more than creative in this category. Companies that stop chasing at two weeks are abandoning most of their pipeline right before it was going to convert.

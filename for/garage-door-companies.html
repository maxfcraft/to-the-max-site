01 / The problem

What goes wrong when garage door companies advertise

Repair demand is urgent and search-driven, and trying to win it in the feed means bidding on a broken spring that has not broken yet.

Replacement is a curb appeal purchase with a real ticket, and it is highly visual, and almost every garage door ad shows a truck instead of a door.

Openers, springs, and smart upgrades are easy, profitable add-ons and almost nobody advertises them as their own offer.

04 / Questions

Questions garage door companies ask

Do Facebook ads work for garage door companies?

Yes, for replacement and upgrades, which are visual curb appeal purchases with a real ticket. For emergency repair the feed is the wrong channel, because a homeowner with a broken spring is on Google right now. Run Meta for replacement and let search catch the breakdowns.

How much does a garage door lead cost?

Forty to one hundred and fifty dollars for replacement leads. On a three thousand dollar install closing at one in three, that is comfortable. Repair leads are cheaper and convert on speed alone, which means the follow-up process matters more than the ad.

What should a garage door ad show?

The door, on a house that looks like the customer's, ideally as a before and after. A new garage door is one of the largest visual changes to a home's front elevation, and that transformation is the entire pitch. A photo of your truck sells nothing.

How do you sell garage door openers and upgrades?

As their own campaign. Smart openers, keypads, and battery backups are low-friction purchases with good margin, and they put a technician in a garage where an aging door gets noticed and quoted. Inside a replacement campaign they never get budget.

Marketing for house cleaning and commercial cleaning companies

01 / The problem

What goes wrong when residential and commercial cleaning companies advertise

The one-time deep clean is the most advertised and least valuable product in the trade. It has a real ticket and no future, and the lead cost has to be recovered in a single visit.

Cleaning is a trust purchase before it is a price purchase. A stranger is getting a key to the house. Ads that lead with a rate and never show a human being are solving the wrong objection.

Commercial and office cleaning is steadier, larger, and barely contested in the feed, and most residential cleaners never run a single campaign at it.

02 / The build

What To The Max builds for residential and commercial cleaning companies

  1. Recurring service as the headline offer, with a first-clean incentive that only unlocks on a recurring schedule.

  2. The team, the background checks, the insurance, and the guarantee made visible, because the real objection is who is in my house, not what does it cost.

  3. Instant online quoting from bedrooms, bathrooms, and square footage, so the lead gets a real number in the first minute instead of waiting for a callback.

  4. A separate commercial campaign aimed at offices, clinics, and property managers, where contracts are larger and the feed is nearly empty.

  5. Move-in and move-out campaigns timed to local listing activity, which is a reliable and underused source of high-ticket one-off work.

04 / Questions

Questions residential and commercial cleaning companies ask

Do Facebook ads work for cleaning companies?

Yes, and the category is well suited to it because the decision is local, emotional, and trust-driven. The campaigns that fail are the ones advertising a one-time deep clean, which has to recover the entire lead cost in a single visit. Advertise the recurring schedule instead.

How much does a house cleaning lead cost?

Thirty to eighty dollars in most markets. Against one deep clean that is uncomfortable. Against a biweekly client worth three thousand dollars a year for two years, it is very cheap. The offer in the ad determines which of those two you are actually buying.

What is the best offer for a cleaning company ad?

A discount on the first clean that only applies when the customer starts a recurring schedule. It gives the ad a reason to act on, and it filters for the customer you want rather than the one-time bargain hunter who will never book again.

How do you overcome the trust problem in cleaning ads?

Show the actual people. Background checks, insurance, a named guarantee, and real photographs of your team do more for conversion in this category than any price. The customer is handing a stranger a key to their home, and that is the objection the ad has to answer first.

Should a cleaning company advertise to commercial clients?

Yes, and it is the most overlooked opportunity in the trade. Offices, medical suites, gyms, and property managers all buy recurring cleaning on contracts far larger than residential, and almost nobody is competing for their attention in the feed.

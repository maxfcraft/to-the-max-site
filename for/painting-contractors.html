Marketing for interior and exterior painting contractors

Painting is a visual, high-ticket, seasonally swinging trade where the difference between a good year and a bad one is usually whether the schedule was booked before the season started.

01 / The problem

What goes wrong when painting contractors advertise

Exterior work has a hard season and interior work does not, and most painting companies advertise as if they are one business, so winter is a cliff every single year.

Free estimate ads produce enormous volume and a calendar full of unpaid driving, because nothing in the offer filters for scope or budget.

Painting is bought with the eyes, and most painting ads show a roller and a tin instead of the finished room the customer is imagining.

02 / The build

What To The Max builds for painting contractors

  1. Interior and exterior as separate campaigns on opposite seasonal schedules, so the business never has an empty quarter.

  2. Estimates qualified in the form: room count or square footage, interior or exterior, and timeline, so the site visit is worth the drive.

  3. Finished-room and finished-house photography as the entire creative direction, shot in homes that look like the target customer's home.

  4. Cabinet refinishing and specialty work run as their own campaign, since the margin is better and the buyer is different.

  5. Commercial and property management campaigns for repaint cycles, which are steady, repeatable, and barely advertised to.

04 / Questions

Questions painting contractors ask

Do Facebook ads work for painting contractors?

Yes, because painting is visual and the ticket is high enough to carry a real lead cost. The mistake is running a generic free estimate offer, which fills the calendar with unpaid site visits instead of jobs. Qualify scope and timeline in the form and the same budget performs completely differently.

How much does a painting lead cost?

Fifty to one hundred and eighty dollars for a qualified lead. On a five thousand dollar interior repaint closing at one in three, that is very comfortable. The expensive lead is the cheap one that turns into a half day of driving for a job that was never real.

How do painters stay busy in winter?

By running interior work as its own campaign on its own schedule. Interior repaints, cabinet refinishing, and trim work are indoor jobs with no weather constraint, and homeowners want them done before the holidays and in the slow months after. The companies that switch ads off in November create their own cliff.

What should a painting ad show?

The finished room or the finished house, in a property that resembles the customer's. Rollers, tins, and crews in white overalls sell nothing, because the customer is not buying the process. They are buying an image of their own house that they already have in their head.

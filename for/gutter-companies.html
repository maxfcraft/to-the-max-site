01 / The problem

What goes wrong when gutter installation and cleaning companies advertise

Demand arrives in spikes tied to weather and leaf drop, and most gutter companies start advertising after the spike, when costs are highest and the calendar is already going to fill anyway.

Cleaning is a small ticket and installation and guards are a large one, and running them in the same campaign guarantees the cheap work eats the budget.

Gutter guards are one of the few local products with a genuine long-term payback story, and almost nobody makes that argument in the ad.

02 / The build

What To The Max builds for gutter installation and cleaning companies

  1. Seasonal campaigns that switch on ahead of leaf drop and ahead of the rainy season, not during them.

  2. Cleaning and installation split into separate campaigns with separate budgets, because a two hundred dollar clean and a three thousand dollar install cannot share an optimisation target.

  3. A guard campaign built on the payback argument: the cost of never cleaning again against the cost of cleaning twice a year forever.

  4. Damage-trigger creative after heavy weather, aimed at the neighbourhoods that actually got hit.

  5. Cleaning customers moved onto a recurring schedule at the visit, which converts the cheapest lead in the trade into a repeating one.

04 / Questions

Questions gutter installation and cleaning companies ask

Do Facebook ads work for gutter companies?

Yes, especially for guards and full replacement, which carry real tickets and a clear payback argument. Cleaning alone is a thin margin against a lead cost, so it works best as an entry point that puts a technician on the roof where the install gets sold.

When should gutter ads run?

Ahead of the triggers, not during them. Live before leaf drop in autumn and before the rainy season in spring. Once the gutters are already overflowing, every competitor is bidding, costs spike, and the calendar was going to fill anyway.

How do you sell gutter guards with ads?

With the payback maths. Two cleanings a year forever against a one-time install is an argument a homeowner can actually check, and it beats any feature list about mesh design. Show the number, show the timeline, and let them do the arithmetic themselves.

Should gutter cleaning and installation share a campaign?

No. A two hundred dollar cleaning lead and a three thousand dollar install lead cannot share an optimisation target, and the algorithm will always find you more of the cheap one. Separate campaigns, separate budgets, judged together at the end of the month.

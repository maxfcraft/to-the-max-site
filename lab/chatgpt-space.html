How to use ChatGPT Space

ChatGPT Space is where your ChatGPT pages live: documents you write, edit with ChatGPT beside you, and share with other paid users. It replaced Library in the left sidebar. Since November 20, 2026, OpenAI has been rolling it out to Plus users aged 18 and over, gradually, so it may not be on your account yet.

Written from OpenAI's documentation. Last checked 21 November 2026.

01 / Who has it

Who has Space, and who does not

  • PlusRolling out since 20 Nov 2026, ages 18+
  • FreeNo Space, cannot open shared pages
  • GoNo Space
  • Extra costNone
  • Build and editWeb and desktop app
  • MobileRead, share, move between pages

If you pay for Plus and Space is not in your sidebar yet, your account is not broken. OpenAI's help article says plainly that it may not be available for your account yet. The rollout is global and gradual.

Press coverage reports that Pro, Business and Enterprise accounts had Space before Plus. Whether full page editing works on mobile is not clearly documented, so plan to build on a computer.

02 / Your first page

Build your first page

Space opens from the left sidebar, in the spot where Library used to be.

  1. Select New page in the sidebar and give the page a title.

  2. Type, paste notes, or add a file. You can also ask ChatGPT to make a page from a conversation you already had, or start from a template on Space home.

  3. Type / to open the insert menu. It holds headings, lists, checklists, quotes, code, tables, dividers, links, images, files, and mentions of other files, pages and chats. Type / then choose Page to make a subpage.

  4. Open ChatGPT beside the page to edit with it. Select any text and a small toolbar appears with Ask for change and Comment.

Group pages into a space

A space is a group of pages. From All, choose New, then Space. Name it, use Invite people if you want company, and select Create.

The views along the top are All, Pages, Suggested, Recents, Favorites, Your items, Shared with you, and Images.

The parts of a page that do work

Prompt
A block that stores a request anyone reading the page can run.
Task and Agent Instructions
The other two agent blocks. OpenAI names them but documents little about how each one behaves, so this guide does not guess.
/visualize
Builds an interactive calculator, diagram or screen design inside the page.
Generate
Writes a draft in place, on the page.
Tables
Each table has Copy link and Copy as Markdown.

A page can draw on notes, files and links you give it. For Google Drive, connect the app and point ChatGPT at the files you want it to use.

03 / The catch

The self-updating catch

This is where most of the confusion sits. Two OpenAI pages say different things about pages that update themselves.

Act on the second one. A page that says “refresh every morning” is a page with a sentence in it. Nothing runs until a schedule exists.

OpenAI's feature page describes where this is heading: an automation with instructions on what to update, which sources to use and how often to run, followed by changes you review. Its examples include a to-do list refreshing hourly, a checklist refreshing hourly from Slack, and a burndown chart updating weekly. OpenAI has not said whether the Keep updated control has reached Plus accounts.

What works today

The agents documentation gives one route: “To set up recurring work, ask ChatGPT in chat and review the saved schedule.” In practice that means five steps.

  1. Build the page first. Give it the sections you want filled in and a line where the date of the last update goes.

  2. Ask in a chat, not on the page. Tell ChatGPT to set up recurring work for that page by name. Say what to change, which sources to use, and when to run.

  3. Review the saved schedule. Check that the day and time match what you asked for.

  4. Confirm it is enabled. A saved task that is switched off does nothing.

  5. Wait for one real run. After the first scheduled time, open the page and confirm it changed. Until it has, treat the page as manual.

OpenAI has not published a list of allowed intervals, a cap on how many pages can update themselves, or whether you are notified after a run. Its examples use hourly, daily and weekly. Which connected apps you can use depends on your account: Google Drive, Slack, email, calendar and Microsoft Teams appear in OpenAI's material, but there is no complete official list.

04 / Templates

Two free starter templates

Each template has three parts: a page skeleton to paste into a new page, the exact request to send in a chat so it runs on a schedule, and a three line check that it really ran. Both use only documented features: pages, pasted notes, checklists, and a schedule set up in chat. Neither needs Sheets.

OpenAI does not say whether Markdown symbols such as ## and - [ ] turn into headings and checklists when pasted. If they stay as plain text, select the block, choose Ask for change, and ask for headings and checklists.

Template 1

The Monday week brief

One page that rebuilds itself every Monday morning from whatever you drop into it during the week. You stop starting the week from a blank screen.

Part 1 · Paste into a new page 
Week brief

Last updated: (ChatGPT writes the date and time here on every run)

## Inbox
Paste anything for the coming week here: meeting notes, emails, dates, half-formed thoughts. ChatGPT clears this section after each run.

## This week at a glance
(ChatGPT fills this in)

## Deadlines and meetings
(ChatGPT fills this in, earliest first, each with its day and date)

## Top priorities
(ChatGPT fills this in)

## Carried over from last week
(ChatGPT fills this in from anything left unfinished)

## Notes
Yours. ChatGPT never edits this section.

## Instructions for ChatGPT
On each run, read the Inbox and everything already on this page.
1. Rewrite "This week at a glance" as five short lines.
2. List every deadline and meeting under "Deadlines and meetings" with its day and date, earliest first.
3. Under "Top priorities", pick the three items that matter most this week and give one line on why for each.
4. Move anything unfinished from last week's priorities into "Carried over from last week".
5. Do not invent tasks, dates or meetings that are not on this page.
6. Clear the Inbox once its contents are used.
7. Never edit the Notes section.
8. Write the current date and time on the "Last updated" line.
Part 2 · Send this in a chat 
Set up a recurring task: every Monday at 7:00 AM my time, update my Space page called "Week brief" by following the "Instructions for ChatGPT" section on that page. When the schedule is saved, show it to me with the exact day, the time, and whether it is enabled.

If you have a calendar connected, add this sentence to the request: Also read my calendar for this Monday through Sunday and add those events under Deadlines and meetings. Which connectors you can use depends on your account.

Part 3 · Check it actually ran
  1. Before Monday, open the saved schedule. It should say Monday, 7:00 AM, enabled.
  2. On Monday after 7:00, open the page. The Last updated line should show that morning.
  3. If the date did not move, nothing ran. Go back to the chat, ask to see the saved task, and fix the time or turn it on.
Template 2

The running to-do page

One list that stays current. You paste raw notes into the Inbox whenever they come up. Each morning ChatGPT turns them into checklist items, files them, and clears out what is done.

Part 1 · Paste into a new page 
Running to-do

Last updated: (ChatGPT writes the date and time here on every run)

## Inbox
Paste raw notes here: meeting notes, a voice memo transcript, a list from your phone. One idea per line works. Full paragraphs work too.

## Today
- [ ] (ChatGPT fills this in)

## This week
- [ ] (ChatGPT fills this in)

## Waiting on someone
- [ ] (ChatGPT fills this in, with who and since when)

## Done
(ChatGPT moves checked items here with the date)

## Instructions for ChatGPT
On each run, read the Inbox.
1. Turn every action in it into one checklist item that starts with a verb.
2. File it under "Today" if it is dated today or marked urgent, under "Waiting on someone" if another person owns the next step, and under "This week" otherwise.
3. Keep any names and dates from the original note.
4. Do not add tasks that are not in the notes. Merge duplicates.
5. Move checked items to "Done" with today's date. Delete Done items older than 14 days.
6. Clear the Inbox once every line is handled.
7. Write the current date and time on the "Last updated" line.
Part 2 · Send this in a chat 
Set up a recurring task: every weekday at 8:00 AM my time, update my Space page called "Running to-do" by following the "Instructions for ChatGPT" section on that page. When the schedule is saved, show it to me with the exact days, the time, and whether it is enabled.

To keep your notes in a Google Drive document instead, connect Google Drive, then replace the first line of the instructions with: On each run, read the Google Drive document named "To-do inbox" and skip any line already on this page. Leave the Inbox section empty.

Part 3 · Check it actually ran
  1. Open the saved schedule. It should say weekdays, 8:00 AM, enabled.
  2. The evening before, paste two test lines into the Inbox: “Call the dentist tomorrow” and “Waiting on Sam for the invoice”.
  3. After 8:00, both should sit in the right sections with the Inbox empty. If not, nothing ran: open the saved task in the chat and fix it.

To keep the instructions in an Agent Instructions block instead of a plain section, type / and insert one. OpenAI does not document how that block changes what ChatGPT does, so these templates rely on the plain section. Either way, the schedule comes from the chat request, never from the page.

05 / Limits

What Space cannot do yet

Sheets
Not live. The help article says Sheets are coming soon. Do not build anything that depends on them yet.
Keep updated
Not available at launch, per the agents documentation. Set schedules up in chat, as described above.
Public links
Not available for pages. You share with named people and set each to view, comment or edit.
Free accounts
Sharing needs a paid account on both sides. A friend on Free cannot open your page.
Export
The only documented export is Copy as Markdown on a table. OpenAI documents no PDF or Word export for pages.
Mobile
Documented for reading, sharing and moving between pages. Build on the web or in the desktop app.
Slides and Sites
Slides are rolling out to eligible accounts, and OpenAI has not said whether Plus is one of them. The feature page mentions publishing a website or web app, but OpenAI has not published a how-to for it.

What sharing does do: select Share on a page, pick people, and set view, comment or edit. People with edit access work on the page with you in real time, subpages inherit the page's access, and collaborators cannot see your private chats or your memory.

06 / Space or Projects

Space or Projects

They are separate features, and both still exist. Space replaced Library, not Projects.

Space is for pages: things you write, keep, edit with ChatGPT and share with other paid users. Projects keep a set of related chats and files together. If what you want at the end is a document another person will read, start a page.

Questions

Questions people are asking about ChatGPT Space

Do I have ChatGPT Space on the Plus plan?

It is rolling out now. OpenAI began rolling Space out globally to Plus users aged 18 and over on November 20, 2026. The rollout is gradual, and the help article says it may not be available for your account yet. When it arrives, Space sits in the left sidebar where Library used to be. It costs nothing extra on top of Plus.

Is ChatGPT Space the same as Projects?

No. They are separate features and both still exist. Space holds pages: documents you write, edit with ChatGPT beside you, and share with other paid users. Projects keep a set of related chats and files together. Space replaced Library, not Projects.

Why did the schedule I wrote into my page never run?

Because writing a schedule into a page does not create one. OpenAI's agents documentation says that writing a cadence in a page or its instructions does not by itself confirm that a task is scheduled. To make a page update on a schedule, ask ChatGPT in a chat to set up the recurring work, then review the saved schedule, confirm it is enabled, and wait for one real run before you trust it.

Is there a Keep updated button in ChatGPT Space?

Not at launch, according to OpenAI. The November 20 release note says pages can be set to update automatically from connected apps and tools, but the agents documentation says Keep Updated is not available at launch. OpenAI has not said when it will appear on Plus accounts. Until it does, the documented route is to ask ChatGPT in chat to set up the recurring work.

Can a friend on the Free plan open a page I shared?

No. Sharing a Space page needs a paid ChatGPT account on both sides, and Free accounts cannot open shared pages. The person you share with also cannot see your private chats or your memory.

Can I share a ChatGPT Space page with a public link?

No. Public links are not available for pages. You share a page with specific people and set each one to view, comment or edit. Subpages inherit the access of the page above them.

Where are Sheets in ChatGPT Space?

Not out yet. OpenAI's help article says Sheets are coming soon. Pages are the document type that is live. Slides are rolling out to eligible accounts, and OpenAI has not said whether Plus is one of them.

Can I edit ChatGPT Space pages on my phone?

Plan on a computer for building. OpenAI documents creating and editing pages on the web and in the desktop app. On mobile, Space is documented for reading, sharing and moving between pages. Whether full editing works on mobile is not clearly documented.

Marketing agency for local service businesses in Huntsville, Alabama

To The Max is a marketing agency for local and home-service businesses, founded and operated by Maximus Fayrweather in Huntsville, Alabama. The agency runs paid advertising on Meta platforms, builds landing pages and websites, sets up lead capture forms and follow-up systems, and produces the content and creative behind the campaigns. Its defining practice is testing every strategy on the founder's own local business before applying it to a client's, and publishing results in a dated public record called the Ledger.

About this agency

Services offered

To The Max provides paid advertising management on Meta (Facebook and Instagram), landing page and website design, lead capture and follow-up systems, and content and creative production. These services are sold as one connected system rather than separate line items, because an ad without a converting page and fast follow-up wastes budget. Details are listed under services.

Who it serves

The agency works with local service businesses and owner-operated companies: home services, trades, artists and studios, and other businesses that win work in their own city. The first clients include a pressure washing company and a commissioned-art studio, both documented on the proof page.

The own-business proof model

The founder owns Supreme Clean, a pressure washing company in Huntsville generating five thousand dollars per month in revenue. Advertising strategies are funded with the founder's own money on that business first. Wins and failures are recorded with dates in the Ledger, which serves as the agency's only self-promotion.

Service area

Based in Huntsville, Alabama, serving the Huntsville metro area and clients across the United States remotely. Contact information is on the contact section.

How engagement works

Engagement starts with a fifteen-minute call or text conversation with the founder. A written plan with the offer, budget, and expected cost per lead comes before any spend. Work runs month to month, and the client owns every ad account, page, and asset from day one. See how to start, and the privacy policy and terms of service for legal details.
